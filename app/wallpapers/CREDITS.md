# Nature photography

Twelve real nature photographs are bundled at 3840 × 2160 pixels for offline use in Quiet Home. The eight additions were downloaded on 2026-10-28 from originals large enough for the 4K crop, without upscaling. Delivered crops were visually reviewed for detail and nature-only content.

- **Layered blue mountain ranges** — Dmytro Koplyk. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/layered-blue-mountain-ranges-fading-into-haze-WvW6pITIJR4).
- **Panther Creek Falls, Washington** — Justin McFadden. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-lush-forest-waterfall-cascades-over-mossy-rocks-TGHAl-YJvUw).
- **Icelandic coast** — Jeremy Bishop. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/aerial-photography-of-coastline-190N6i-Rdb0).
- **Desert dunes at sunset** — Wolfgang Hasselmann. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/desert-dunes-at-sunset-with-gradient-sky-I3o0Y01kke8).
- **Dolomites sunrise** — Marek Piwnicki. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/snow-capped-mountains-bathed-in-golden-sunrise-light-8JFuGoEFI5I). Original dimensions: 5296 × 3276.
- **Turquoise coastline** — Rod Long. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/aerial-view-of-a-sandy-coastline-with-shallow-turquoise-water-iqBc91jdqoQ). Original dimensions: 8102 × 5401.
- **Autumn forest canopy** — Denis Mollaymeri. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/autumn-forest-with-colorful-trees-on-a-hillside-3dajdLjvRBA). Original dimensions: 4536 × 8064.
- **Northern lights** — David Becker. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/aurora-borealis-over-body-of-water-during-nighttime-58X3XfxxevU). Original dimensions: 4840 × 3206.
- **Glacial lagoon** — Danny Rienecker. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/icebergs-floating-in-a-glacial-lagoon-with-mountains-behind-9v9uIJKEhDY). Original dimensions: 6000 × 4000.
- **White Sands at sunset** — Joseph Corl. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/the-sun-is-setting-over-a-vast-expanse-of-sand-dunes-h4eHV9CRxUc). Original dimensions: 7637 × 5091.
- **Sunlit forest** — Josef Hejpetr. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/sunbeams-shining-through-a-lush-green-forest-ovPxIpIZotw). Original dimensions: 4081 × 2721.
- **Ocean swell** — Milin John. [Original photograph](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-large-wave-crashes-on-a-sandy-beach-Go811IU9a2g). Original dimensions: 5250 × 3500.

All source pages identify these photos as free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), rather than Unsplash+. Exact download URLs and file hashes are in `credits.json`. Picker thumbnails are separate 640 × 360 versions; they are never used as the full-screen photograph.
